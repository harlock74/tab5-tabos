# Third-party BASIC examples

## AMAZING

`AMAZING.bas` is the normalized original BASIC listing for **Amazing**, written
by Jack Hauber and published in David H. Ahl's *BASIC Computer Games*. It was
retrieved unchanged from `coding-horror/basic-computer-games` commit
`5301155192d91d74d337899cecc59dbda59c4c17`:

<https://github.com/coding-horror/basic-computer-games/blob/5301155192d91d74d337899cecc59dbda59c4c17/02_Amazing/amazing.bas>

That repository releases its contents under the Unlicense. The exact license text
is retained in `UNLICENSE` beside this notice. `AMAZING.prg` is the same listing in
TabBASIC's ordinary tokenized SAVE format; no source line was changed.

`C64DODGE` and every other example in this directory are project-authored unless
their source or this notice says otherwise.
